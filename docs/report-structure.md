# Roots Local · Full DNA report outline

Stable chapter and section IDs used in the app, PDF, and catalog. The outline describes the intended scope. Each result depends on source data, available rules, and evidence.

## Part I · Ancestry, family, and your genome


### 01 · Ancestry, origins & human evolution

- **01.1 Geographic ancestry** · Ancestry estimates, regions, genetic groups, maps, regional history, and suitable reference panel comparisons.
- **01.2 Ancestry along chromosomes** · Segment-level ancestry, comparisons with global estimates, competing assignments, and uncertainty.
- **01.3 Direct maternal and paternal lines** · Mitochondrial and Y-DNA haplogroups, lineage trees, geographic context, and resolution.
- **01.4 Ancient and archaic ancestry** · Imported Ancient Origins results, comparisons with archaeological reference genomes, and Neanderthal or Denisovan ancestry where appropriate methods exist.
- **01.5 Evolution and adaptation** · Historical adaptations such as milk consumption and altitude, with population context.

### 02 · Relatives, shared DNA & inheritance

- **02.1 DNA matches** · Imported matches, shared centimorgans, segment counts and lengths, and possible relationships.
- **02.2 Chromosome comparison** · Shared and triangulated segments, shared matches, and family-branch groups.
- **02.3 Parental and family inheritance** · Assignment to a parental side, comparisons with relatives, and inheritance of variants when supporting files are available.
- **02.4 Genetic diversity** · Homozygous and heterozygous positions, runs of homozygosity, and comparisons with appropriate references.

### 03 · Your genome, variants & data quality

- **03.1 File and testing method** · Provider, raw format, array or sequencing, reference assembly, marker count, chromosomes, and missing genotypes.
- **03.2 Variant types** · SNPs, small insertions and deletions, structural variants, copy number, repeat expansions, mitochondrial variants, and heteroplasmy when available.
- **03.3 Location and function** · Genes, transcripts, coding and regulatory regions, protein consequences, RNA splicing, and variants on the same copy.
- **03.4 Coverage** · Usable, missing, ambiguous, or conflicting positions, CRAM depth and quality, and measured or imputed genotypes where stated.
## Part II · Body, senses, and everyday traits


### 04 · Appearance, skin, hair & body shape

- **04.1 Pigmentation** · Eye, hair, and skin color, freckles, tanning, and sunburn tendency.
- **04.2 Hair and skin traits** · Hair texture, graying, hair loss, skin barrier and dryness, skin aging, wound healing, scars, and keloids.
- **04.3 Face and visible traits** · Facial and cranial proportions, nose, chin, ears, and earwax type.
- **04.4 Growth and body composition** · Height, proportions, body weight, fat distribution, muscle mass, and lean mass.

### 05 · Senses, perception & motor traits

- **05.1 Taste and smell** · Bitter taste, cilantro, asparagus odor, olfactory receptors, and individual odorants.
- **05.2 Vision** · Myopia, hyperopia, color vision, retina, lens, optic nerve, glaucoma, and other inherited eye traits.
- **05.3 Hearing and balance** · Hearing, hearing loss, tinnitus, inherited hearing disorders, and motion sickness.
- **05.4 Motor and sensory traits** · Handedness, coordination, temperature perception, pain sensitivity, and sensory processing.

### 06 · Sport, muscles, bones & movement

- **06.1 Muscle biology** · ACTN3, muscle fiber function, muscle metabolism, grip strength, and muscle mass.
- **06.2 Performance and exertion** · Studies on sprinting, strength, endurance, oxygen uptake, training response, and recovery.
- **06.3 Bones, joints, and connective tissue** · Bone density, osteoporosis, fractures, osteoarthritis, tendons, ligaments, Dupuytren disease, and inherited connective tissue disorders.
- **06.4 Everyday activity** · Organized and everyday movement, sedentary behavior, and sensor measurements as research traits.

### 07 · Nutrition, tolerance & metabolism

- **07.1 Digestion of food components** · Lactase activity, population-dependent markers, starch and other food components, and references to celiac disease and allergies.
- **07.2 Caffeine, alcohol, and other compounds** · Metabolism, enzyme function, and alcohol flushing.
- **07.3 Vitamins, minerals, and trace elements** · Vitamin D, B vitamins, iron uptake and storage, and other documented micronutrients.
- **07.4 Sugar, fats, and energy balance** · Glucose, insulin, type 2 diabetes, cholesterol, triglycerides, appetite, satiety, and weight.
- **07.5 Gut microbiome** · Research on human genetic variants and microbiome composition, plus optional separately measured data.

### 08 · Sleep, behavior, cognition & aging

- **08.1 Sleep and the body clock** · Chronotype, sleep duration and timing, insomnia, snoring, and sleep apnea.
- **08.2 Personality and behavior** · Research on the Big Five, habits, tobacco, alcohol, other substance use, and dependence.
- **08.3 Memory, attention, and learning** · Research on memory, attention, reaction time, and other cognitive traits.
- **08.4 Aging and lifespan** · Telomeres, lifespan, healthy aging, age-related traits, and optional measured biomarkers.
## Part III · Health by body system


### 09 · Heart, vessels, blood & breathing

- **09.1 Heart** · Heart attack, coronary artery disease, rhythm, atrial fibrillation, cardiomyopathy, and heart structure.
- **09.2 Vessels and circulation** · Blood pressure, arterial and venous disorders, vascular structure, and stroke.
- **09.3 Blood clotting** · Thrombosis, bleeding tendency, and inherited clotting disorders.
- **09.4 Blood counts and blood groups** · Red and white blood cells, hemoglobin, platelets, ABO, Rh, and other groups where data permit.
- **09.5 Lungs and airways** · Lung function, COPD, other airway conditions, and references to asthma, sleep-related breathing, and exposures.

### 10 · Digestion, kidneys, hormones & other systems

- **10.1 Mouth, teeth, and gums** · Cavities, periodontitis, enamel, and inherited tooth traits.
- **10.2 Stomach and bowel** · Reflux, irritable bowel syndrome, diverticular disease, inflammatory bowel disease, and celiac disease.
- **10.3 Liver, gallbladder, and pancreas** · Liver enzymes, fatty liver, iron overload, gallstones, and pancreatic disorders.
- **10.4 Kidneys, urinary tract, and pelvic floor** · Kidney function, stones, uric acid, gout, bladder, urinary tract, and pelvic floor.
- **10.5 Hormones and endocrine organs** · Thyroid, adrenal glands, hormonal axes, hormone levels, and links to insulin.
- **10.6 Dermatology** · Acne, psoriasis, atopic and other skin conditions, skin barrier, and healing.

### 11 · Immunity, inflammation, allergies & infections

- **11.1 Immunogenetics** · HLA, antigen presentation, immune regulation, antibodies, and cytokines.
- **11.2 Allergies and atopy** · Hay fever, atopic dermatitis, allergic asthma, and other allergic predispositions.
- **11.3 Autoimmunity** · Celiac disease, type 1 diabetes, thyroid autoimmunity, rheumatologic conditions, and other autoimmune diseases.
- **11.4 Susceptibility and immune response** · Observed associations between human variants, susceptibility to infection, and immune measurements.
- **11.5 Inherited immune disorders and lymphatics** · Inborn immune disorders, complement, primary lymphedema, and lymphatic vessels.

### 12 · Brain, nerves, pain & mental health

- **12.1 Neurology** · Migraine and subtypes, epilepsy, movement disorders, inherited neurologic conditions, Parkinson's and Alzheimer's diseases.
- **12.2 Pain and sensory processing** · Chronic pain, neuropathic pain, and pain sensitivity.
- **12.3 Neurodevelopment** · Research on developmental traits, neurodevelopmental conditions, and rare syndromes.
- **12.4 Mental health** · Research on depression, anxiety, other psychiatric conditions, and shared genetic influences.

### 13 · Reproduction, pregnancy & development

- **13.1 Reproductive health** · Reproductive organs, fertility, endometriosis, menstrual traits, and menopause.
- **13.2 Pregnancy and birth** · Pregnancy traits, birth weight, and genetic contributions from the mother and child.
- **13.3 Congenital and early development** · Organ development, growth, developmental syndromes, and multisystem traits.
- **13.4 Family planning and carrier status** · Recessive and X-linked variants, inheritance, tested positions, and confirmed comparison results when available.
## Part IV · Medical genetics and drugs


### 14 · Inherited conditions, cancer genetics & disease risk

- **14.1 Inherited conditions and inheritance patterns** · Monogenic conditions, dominant, recessive, X-linked and mitochondrial inheritance, and variant-specific evaluations.
- **14.2 Rare disease groups** · Inborn errors of metabolism, lysosomal and peroxisomal disorders, mitochondrial energy production, multisystem syndromes, and conditions involving complex variants.
- **14.3 Cancer genetics** · Inherited predisposition, familial syndromes, organ-specific studies, and separately imported confirmed findings.
- **14.4 Common conditions and polygenic scores** · Models by condition, genotype coverage, reference population, calibration, and links to relevant organ chapters.

### 15 · Drugs, metabolism & anesthesia

- **15.1 Pharmacogenetic profile** · Genotype, haplotype, diplotype, copy number, enzyme activity, and drug transport.
- **15.2 Drug groups** · Cardiovascular drugs, anticoagulants, pain medicines, psychiatric, oncology, immune, and anti-infective treatments.
- **15.3 Adverse effects** · Documented variants, HLA types, and their associated drugs.
- **15.4 Anesthesia** · Selected genetic associations with reactions to anesthetics and requirements for confirmed findings.
## Part V · Molecular research and associations


### 16 · Lab values, proteins, metabolites & imaging

- **16.1 Clinical lab values** · Blood counts, lipids, glucose, liver, kidneys, hormones, vitamins, minerals, inflammation, and optional measured values.
- **16.2 Proteins and signaling molecules** · Proteome, immune proteins, cytokines, and other protein markers.
- **16.3 Metabolites and lipids** · Metabolome, amino acids, small metabolites, and the lipidome.
- **16.4 Glycobiology** · Sugar structures on proteins, including antibody glycosylation.
- **16.5 Organ structure and medical imaging** · Research on MRI, heart, brain, and retinal measurements, with optional personal measurements.

### 17 · Gene regulation, epigenetics & environment

- **17.1 Gene activity by tissue** · Gene expression, eQTLs, and tissue-specific associations.
- **17.2 RNA processing and splicing** · Transcripts, sQTLs, and associations with protein levels and clinical findings.
- **17.3 Epigenetics** · DNA methylation associations and optional separately measured methylation data.
- **17.4 Genes and environment** · Nutrition, movement, tobacco smoke, air pollution, altitude, and optional exposure information.
- **17.5 Variants associated with several traits** · Pleiotropy, traits that span organ systems, and links to related findings.

## Report layout

- Cover, report date, provider, report version, and catalog version.
- Summary, clickable contents, and PDF bookmarks.
- All 17 chapters with individual results, research associations, and coverage gaps.
- Data source, coverage, status, evidence, method, and references for every finding.
- One primary location per finding with cross-references and a searchable index by trait, disease, gene, and variant.
- Research appendix, coverage, methodology, model versions, and sources.
